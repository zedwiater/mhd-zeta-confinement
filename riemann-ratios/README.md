# Riemann Ratios — first round (2026-11-16)

A subdirectory of the zeta program. In the parent catalog's flow
table (../SYSTEM_CATALOG.md), the universe's traffic splits into
two columns: **power** (energy, NS-routed) and **data** (pattern,
Riemann-accounted). This directory is where the data column gets
its bookkeeping: the **ratios** — the dimensionless numbers that
stay constant while everything they relate is changing, and that
therefore carry the pattern from one tier to the next.

## The working claim

Every natural system in the catalog is a superfluidic or solidic
particle–wave interaction, organized as layered encasement:
bilateral parent flows encase daughter flows until a daughter
stabilizes in its own interference pattern. A daughter's right to
exist at its tier — its traversal range, its nesting depth — is an
**aptitude assignment earned by balance**, and balance is measured
in ratios: flow/backflow, shedding/building, coherence/disturbance
(the *calm ratio*). Find the ratios that survive, and you have
found the pattern. Find them at several scales, bound in threes
(**ratio tripoles**), and you have the pattern's grammar.

## Discipline

Natural data only (the parent catalog's sources). Holes are
printed at the same size as findings. Framing statements from the
program's author are labeled as framing and kept separate from
graded results; effects and mechanisms are graded separately, and
a result whose control also passes is reported as an artifact,
not a discovery. Nothing here is a published claim — it is a
working round, versioned so later rounds can correct it in public.
