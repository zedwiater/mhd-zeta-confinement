## The proposal (verbatim)

"use the thermal spectrum of the superposition atmosphere and
its inverse phase offset to form stable equilibrium and durable
superposition with just light from x temp"

## What it claims, clause by clause

1. **The atmosphere has a thermal spectrum, and it is a working
   input, not a byproduct.** The superposition atmosphere (the
   sacrificed-majority medium of §21, the Jar's working
   projection) carries a thermal spectrum that can be read and
   used — the same class of data as Zone 1's target, the thermal
   spectrum of qubit processing.
2. **The atmosphere has an inverse phase offset, and it is the
   second working input.** Paired against the thermal spectrum,
   the offset is what the light flow acts on: spectrum and
   inverse offset together are the control surface.
3. **From the two, stable equilibrium and durable superposition
   — with just light.** No other working input: the light flow
   (flattened flow twisting, or switching, per the Zone 2
   statement) forms the equilibrium and the superposition
   persists — durable, not transient.
4. **From x temp.** The whole system sits at a bath temperature
   (x temp, the author's variable) at or beyond the threshold of
   the companion claim (2026-11-18 20:55 ET): a minimal system
   temperature exists at which light flow alone can bring the
   system to the superposition gradient against the bath.

## Falsifiers (declared with the proposal)

- **Threshold test:** sweep bath temperature at fixed light
  flow. The proposal requires a tip point — equilibrium forms
  and superposition holds on light alone at/beyond x temp, and
  does not above it. A smooth threshold-less decline kills the
  "from x temp" clause as stated.
- **Offset test:** remove or scramble the inverse phase offset
  while holding the thermal spectrum and light flow constant.
  If equilibrium and durability are unchanged, the offset is not
  a working input and clause 2 dies.
- **Spectrum test:** substitute a different thermal spectrum at
  the same total power. If outcomes are identical, the spectrum
  is not a working input and clause 1 dies.
- **§21 inheritance:** hold quality vs sacrifice ratio must
  still show its threshold in any atmosphere built this way;
  flat ratio-dependence kills the population mechanism
  underneath the proposal.
- **Durability test:** the superposition must persist, not
  merely form — a formed-then-decayed gradient grades as
  formation only, and the "durable" clause dies.
