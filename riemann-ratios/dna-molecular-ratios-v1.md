**TELOMERE SHEDDING RATIO (derived, declared arithmetic).**
The program has a live telomere-shedding thread (principles
monitor Q7, where "shedding" is a sim-side word). The
molecular instance, computed here from the sourced rows only:

  shedding ratio per division = loss per division ÷ standing length
  = (50–100 bp) ÷ (9,500–12,000 bp)
  = **(4.2–10.5)×10⁻³ per division, central ≈ 7×10⁻³**

i.e. a newborn-length telomere sheds roughly **0.4–1.1% of
itself per division**. Two structural notes, both arithmetic,
not interpretation: (1) the absolute loss is ~constant while
the denominator shrinks, so the FRACTIONAL shedding rate
grows hyperbolically as the telomere shortens — the ratio is
a function of state, not a constant; (2) the naive division
budget (standing length ÷ loss ≈ 100–240 divisions to zero)
overstates the real Hayflick limit (40–60) because senescence
is triggered by the SHORTEST telomeres in the cell reaching a
critical state, not by the mean reaching zero (Hemann et al.,
Cell 107:67–77 (2001)) — mean-length arithmetic is an upper
bound, printed as such. Cross-check that does close: lifetime
loss 2–4 kb ÷ 50–100 bp/PD = 20–80 PDs, bracketing the
Hayflick range. FRAMING SEPARATION (declared): this is the
molecular literature's attrition, measured. Whether it is the
same "shedding" the Q7 sim thread tracks is the program's
hypothesis, ungraded, and is NOT asserted by this table.

ladder — consecutive nucleosomes keep rotational phase with
the duplex they wrap, so the pitch of Layer 0 is the tick
mark of Layer 1.

|---|---|---|---|---|

| Collagen triple helix | **Gly-X-Y: period 3 residues**, 3 chains, one-residue stagger; helical symmetry **10/3 (3.33 residues/turn, 28.6 Å repeat)** in high-resolution crystal structures, **7/2 (3.5 residues/turn, 20.0 Å repeat)** in other structures — the literature carries BOTH models, unresolved | 2.86 Å/residue | 28.6 Å (10/3) or 20.0 Å (7/2) | Shoulders & Raines, Annu. **The heptad near-miss (derived, mechanism sourced).** Two
turns of an undistorted α-helix hold 2 × 3.63 = 7.26 residues;
the coiled-coil repeat holds exactly 7. The deficit is
7 ÷ 7.26 = 0.964, an angular shortfall of ≈25.8° per heptad
(derived: 720° − (7 ÷ 3.63) × 360°). That accumulating
shortfall is not an error term — per Crick (1953) it is what
forces the helices to wind around each other in a left-handed
supercoil so the hydrophobic stripe stays in register. A
NEAR-integer relation whose small residue does mechanical
work: the nesting map (§5) grades it accordingly.

**Pairwise ratios of the family (derived):**
α ÷ coiled-coil = 3.6/3.5 = **1.029**; α ÷ collagen(10/3) =
3.6/3.333 = **1.080**; coiled-coil ÷ collagen(10/3) =
3.5/3.333 = **1.050**; α ÷ β = 3.6/2 = **1.80**; collagen
triplet period ÷ β period = 3/2 = **1.50**. Rise cross-check:
β-strand rise (3.5 Å) vs DNA rise per bp (3.4 Å) differ by
≈3% — printed as an arithmetic observation, significance
ungraded. Collagen's alternative model is the rational **7/2**
— the same rational as the coiled-coil heptad and as Layer
0's codons-per-turn (3.5). Three appearances, one rational;
see the nesting map for what is and is not claimed.

## Layer 3 — fold spectra (folded vs NOT folded, as ordered)

Two spectroscopies grade protein conformation in practice.
Their band positions are below, converted to frequency with
ν = c·ν̃ (c = 2.998×10¹⁰ cm/s) for FTIR and ν = c/λ for CD —
declared arithmetic, used by the span update in §6.

### FTIR — amide I band (1600–1700 cm⁻¹ ⇒ 4.80–5.10×10¹³ Hz)

| Structure class | Amide I position | Frequency (derived) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| α-helix | **1650–1658 cm⁻¹** | ≈4.95–4.97×10¹³ Hz (at 1654: 4.96×10¹³ Hz) | Barth, Biochim. Biophys. Acta 1767:1073–1101 (2007); Kong & Yu, Acta Biochim. Biophys. Sin. 39:549–559 (2007); assignment table in Garidel & Schott, BioProcess Int. (2006) |
| β-sheet | **1620–1640 cm⁻¹** (plus a high-frequency component ≈1670–1695 cm⁻¹ for antiparallel sheets) | ≈4.86–4.92×10¹³ Hz (at 1630: 4.89×10¹³ Hz) | Same sources |
| Disordered / non-ordered | **1640–1650 cm⁻¹**, broad | ≈4.92–4.95×10¹³ Hz (at 1645: 4.93×10¹³ Hz) | Garidel & Schott (2006) assignment table (1640–1650 = non-ordered) |
| Aggregated / intermolecular β | strong absorption **below ≈1620 cm⁻¹** correlates with aggregation | <4.86×10¹³ Hz | "Molecular spectroscopic markers of abnormal protein aggregation," Int. J. Mol. Sci. (2020), compiling Barth (2007) |

Derived discrimination ratio: α-band centre ÷ β-band centre
= 1654 ÷ 1630 = **1.015** — the two main fold classes are
separated by only ≈1.5% in frequency, and the disordered band
(1645) sits BETWEEN the β centre and the α centre, ≈0.5%
below α. Fold-class readout from amide I is therefore a
deconvolution exercise on overlapping bands, not a set of
resolved lines; that overlap is a property of the data,
printed here rather than smoothed over.

### Circular dichroism — far-UV signatures

| Structure class | CD signature | Photon frequency (derived, c/λ) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| α-helix | negative bands at **222 and 208 nm**, positive at **193 nm** | 222 nm → 1.35×10¹⁵ Hz; 208 nm → 1.44×10¹⁵ Hz; 193 nm → 1.55×10¹⁵ Hz | Greenfield, Nat. Protoc. 1:2876–2890 (2006) |
| β-sheet | negative at **≈218 nm** (215–218), positive at **195–200 nm** | 218 nm → 1.38×10¹⁵ Hz | Greenfield (2006) |
| Disordered / random coil | very low ellipticity above 210 nm, negative band near **195–198 nm** | 195 nm → 1.54×10¹⁵ Hz | Greenfield (2006) |

**The one spectral RATIO in routine use: [θ]₂₂₂/[θ]₂₀₈.**
Values **≥ 1.0 indicate interacting helices (coiled-coil)**;
values **≤ 0.86 indicate isolated single helices** (Lau,
Taneja & Hodges, J. Biol. Chem. 259:13253–13261 (1984); in
active use e.g. McNamara et al., Science 319:1405–1408
(2008)). PRECISION FLAG (declared): this ratio grades
helix–helix INTERACTION, not foldedness as such — an isolated
but perfectly folded helix reads ≈0.86. It is the closest
thing in the sourced literature to a single-number spectral
fold-state ratio, and its actual referent is narrower than
"folded vs not."

**BOUNDARY (labeled, as ordered).** What spectroscopy grades
directly is STRUCTURE-CLASS CONTENT (fractions of helix /
sheet / disorder from band deconvolution). "Folded vs NOT
folded" as a verdict is in practice a THERMODYNAMIC quantity
— melting temperature Tm and folding free energy ΔG from
thermal or denaturant melts — usually MONITORED through a
spectral probe (ellipticity at 222 nm is the standard
follow signal) but not itself a spectral position or ratio.
No sourced single spectral ratio grades folded-vs-unfolded
across protein classes; anyone selling one is ahead of the
literature. That absence is recorded in the holes (§7).

**Calm-analog candidates (SPECULATION, labeled as such —
this mapping is Muse's, offered to the program, not found
in the molecular literature).** In the Earth and quantum
tables, calm = persistence of pattern ÷ duration of the
disturbing event (Schumann coherence ÷ light round trip;
T2 ÷ gate time). Two molecular ratios have that shape:

1. **Base-pair breathing ratio:** closed lifetime ÷ open
   lifetime ≈ 5 ms ÷ 100 ns ≈ **5×10⁴** (range 10⁴–10⁵ on
   the sourced endpoints). A base pair's information-bearing
   state persists ~10⁴–10⁵ times longer than each disruption
   event lasts. Structurally the closest analog to T2/gate
   time in this stack.
2. **Telomere buffer ratio:** standing length ÷ loss per
   division ≈ **100–240** (the reciprocal of the shedding
   ratio, §0) — the number of disturbance events the buffer
   can absorb before exhaustion, before the shortest-telomere
   correction cuts it to the observed 40–60. A coherence
   window denominated in shedding events.

No other Layer-4 ratio is claimed as a calm analog. Protein
folding time ÷ speed limit (≈2–12× for the fastest folders,
~10⁴× for a median protein against its own limit) measures
distance from an ideal, not persistence against disturbance,
and is NOT promoted to the calm role here.

## 7. Holes — printed at the same size as the findings

- **No sourced single spectral ratio grades folded vs
  unfolded across protein classes.** The 222/208 ratio grades
  helix interaction; Tm/ΔG grade foldedness but are
  thermodynamic, not spectral (§3 boundary). The requested
  "foldedness ratio" does not exist in the literature as a
  universal number.
- **Telomere standing length is instrument-split.** TRF,
  flow FISH and Q-FISH disagree by ≈25% at birth (9.5 vs
  11.0 vs 11.9 kb midpoints). The shedding ratio inherits
  that spread; it is reported as a range for this reason.
- **Collagen's own house is divided:** 10/3 vs 7/2 helical
  symmetry both stand in the peer-reviewed literature
  (Shoulders & Raines 2009). This table carries both; the
  pairwise ratios in §2 use 10/3 and say so.
- **147 vs 146:** the canonical core-particle length is
  147 bp; the 1997 crystal structure itself contained 146 bp.
  Both printed (§1); derived turns use 147.
- **DNA melting** is graded by a temperature (Tm), not a
  timescale or a ratio; no sourced "melting rate" row is
  offered, and none was found in a form comparable to the
  breathing rows.
- **Folding times for large / multi-domain proteins** are
  seconds-class and heterogeneous; no single sourced ladder
  from µs to s exists — the table gives the speed limit, the
  fastest folders, and the two-state median, and leaves the
  rest of the range a labeled gap rather than a smooth line.
- **A320 / turbidity-style foldedness checks** were named in
  the order as an example; no sourced ratio form was verified
  this pass, so none is tabled. Aggregation's sourced
  spectral marker (sub-1620 cm⁻¹ β band) is tabled instead.
