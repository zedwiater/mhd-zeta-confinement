## §2 SOLIDIC SIDE — the power column (inverse transduced projections)

### §2a The tidal constituents are beat daughters — the arithmetic

Every tidal period below is a beat (sum/difference) of three
parent rotations: Earth's spin (sidereal day 23.93447 h), the
Moon's orbital circuit (sidereal month 27.3217 d), and Earth's
year. Declared arithmetic, frequencies in cycles/hour:

- Parent rates: spin f_s = 1/23.93447 = 0.0417807; lunar orbit
  f_m = 1/(27.3217 × 24) = 0.0015250; year f_y = 1/(365.256 × 24)
  = 0.0001141.
- **The lunar day** = 1/(f_s − f_m) = 1/0.0402557 = **24.8412 h**
  — the Moon's apparent circuit of the sky, spin beating orbit.
- **M2 = half the lunar day = 12.4206 h.** Cross-check against
  the official constant: NOAA lists M2's angular speed as
  28.984104°/h; 360 ÷ 28.984104 = 12.42060 h. The beat
  arithmetic reproduces the official period to 5 significant
  figures.
- **S2 = half the solar day**: 1/(f_s − f_y) = 24.0000 h → S2 =
  **12.0000 h** (NOAA speed exactly 30.000000°/h).
- **Spring–neap cycle = the M2×S2 beat**: 1/|2(f_s − f_m) −
  2(f_s − f_y)| → 1/0.0028220 = 354.4 h = **14.765 d = exactly
  half the synodic month** (29.5306 ÷ 2 = 14.7653). Springs and
  neaps are the synodic cycle read through the tide.
- **N2 = M2 split by the anomalistic (perigee) rate**:
  f_anom = 1/(27.55455 × 24) = 0.0015121; 1/(0.0805113 −
  0.0015121) = **12.6584 h** ✓ (standard table value).
- **K1 = the sidereal day itself**: 1/f_s = 23.9345 h (table:
  23.9344 h) — the declinational daughter, Earth's spin read
  once per day against the Moon's tilted orbit.
- **O1 = 25.8193 h** (table value; NOAA speed 13.943035°/h) —
  the principal lunar diurnal: the lunar-day beat further split
  by the nodal (draconic) regression. Its exact Doodson
  parentage is a multi-term difference; the period is tabled
  from the standard development rather than force-derived here.

**THE SPLIT VERDICT (the table's thesis, with its receipts).**
The Moon returns **~10⁻⁶ of the Sun's light** (§1: flux ratio
10⁻⁶ class; albedo 0.11–0.12) and does **3.2 TW of tidal
work** — itself only 1.8×10⁻⁵ of the 175,000 TW of sunlight
Earth intercepts (derived from Munk & Wunsch 1998). Two
whispers. But they are not the same kind of whisper. The power
column's 3.2 TW runs continuously and buys bulk motion:
oceans lifted twice a day, a planet's spin braked at
+1.78 ms/century, a satellite captured into 1:1 lock. The data
column peaks at ≈0.13 TW of intercepted moonlight for a few
nights a month (derived: ~10⁻³ W/m² full-moon irradiance ×
Earth's intercept area π R⊕² = 1.275×10¹⁴ m²; cycle-averaged
an order lower) — roughly **1/25 of the tidal wattage at its
peak, ~1/300 cycle-averaged** — and buys no bulk motion at
all. What it buys is SYNCHRONY: entrained clocks in worms,
corals, midges and plankton across an ocean basin, a
polarization compass read by a beetle at 10⁻⁶ of daylight, and
a measurable 46–58-minute modulation of human sleep. **Flux
is the wrong meter for the data column; entrainment reach is
the right one — and by that meter the 10⁻⁶ channel is the
Moon's larger legacy on Earth.** Zak's bifurcation question,
answered in the table's own terms: moonlight played almost no
role as power in Earth's local light evolution, and a leading
role as pattern.

## §5 HOLES — printed at the same size as findings

- **Geological moonlight brightness is two anchors and a
  model.** Measured: today (LLR) and 1.4 Gyr ago (Meyers &
  Malinverno 2018, ×1.27). Everything earlier — including the
  ×145–404 formation-era range in §1 — rests on giant-impact
  model distances (3–5 R⊕), not measurement. No instrument
  reads palaeo-moonlight; rhythmites constrain distance and
  day length, from which brightness is only derived.
- **Polarization vs phase has no unified curve.** Reported
  lunar-skylight DoLP maxima span 0.25–0.8 across instruments,
  wavelength bands, sites and phases (Barta 2014; Kyba 2011;
  Foster 2019). The phase-dependence is established
  qualitatively (smaller illuminated fraction → lower DoLP,
  Foster et al. 2019) but no single instrument has published a
  full phase-resolved DoLP curve for the moonlit sky. The
  spread in §1 is the honest state of the literature.
- **No global observed tidal amplitude ratio exists.** §2b's
  ratios are equilibrium (forcing) ratios. Realized ratios are
  per-coast fingerprints (NOAA station constants differ by an
  order of magnitude between nearby stations); a worldwide
  "observed S2/M2" is not a number the literature holds.
- **Body tide vs ocean tide has no like-for-like ratio.** The
  bridge is the Love number h₂ ≈ 0.61 (§2c), not a quotient of
  the headline amplitudes; any table that prints one has mixed
  an all-constituent maximum with a single-constituent
  amplitude.
- **A terrestrial circalunar clock at Platynereis grade was
  searched for and not found.** The strong clock receipts are
  marine (worm, midge, coral-by-phase) plus one human
  behavioural modulation (sleep). The dung beetle reads the
  Moon as a compass, not a calendar. Whether land animals run
  true endogenous circalunar clocks remains open in the
  literature, not merely unfilled here.
- **The menstrual daughter is absent, and its absence is
  load-bearing.** The near-coincidence of mean cycle length
  (29.3 d) with the synodic month (29.53 d) is the most-cited
  "moon ratio" in popular culture; at population scale it does
  not survive its own dataset (§3). Kept in the table as a
  BROKEN daughter, per house rules — failures are kept,
  marked, never deleted.
- **Deep-time biological daughters are ungradeable.** No
  fossil or molecular receipt yet grades whether circalunar
  clocks predate the Cambrian, when nights were shorter, the
  Moon closer and brighter (×1.27 at 1.4 Gyr, more earlier),
  and the lunar day nearer 18.7 h than 24.84 h. The daughters
  in §3 evolved under a parent whose parameters have drifted
  by tens of percent; the drift is measured (§1, §2c), its
  evolutionary bookkeeping is a hole.
